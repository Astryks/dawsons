A free online DAW that stays in your tab

MyDawsons is a free browser music studio. The drum kits are public-domain and CC0 one-shots plus a few original synth drums. The piano and other melodic sounds are either built in this page or streamed from freely licensed sample sets (Steinway recordings in the public domain, CC0 and CC-BY instruments). Nothing you play is uploaded to a Dawsons server.

The studio is the simple path: an Akai-style pad grid, a mini keyboard, transport with loop, and a prompt that writes a starter arrangement. The classic DAW is still there if you want the older timeline, mixer, DJ decks, and in-house arranger.

Voice, without a cloud model

Pitch is tracked in your browser and played back through a synth or sampler while you sing. Autotune snaps that to a scale. Voice-changer presets (robot, radio, cave, chipmunk, and more) are ordinary Web Audio effects on your mic. Read more on voice to instrument.

Splitting a song

Upload a file you have the rights to. The studio can quick-split it in the tab into drums, bass, melody, and the rest. A real vocal separation uses the open-source Demucs model in the local Dawsons engine — you run that yourself; this website does not call a paid API. The desktop app still does the full analysis.
