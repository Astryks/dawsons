Dawsons GitHub

The DAW that understands music, not just plays it back.

Upload any song and get it back as an editable project — stems, tempo, key, chords, structure. Sing an idea and turn it into any instrument. All running locally, on a fully free software stack.

View the project See what it does
◐

Song → editable project

Drop in a track and Dawsons separates it into stems, detects BPM, key, chords, and section structure — laid out on a timeline you can actually edit.

◑

Sing it, hear any instrument

Hum or sing a melody and render it back as piano, guitar, synth, or anything else — the performance stays yours, the instrument doesn't have to.

◒

Reverse & re-pitch anything

Grab any clip, flip it backwards, and pitch it up or down to find a texture that was never in the original — the classic trick behind a lot of sounds you already know.

◓

Built on confidence, not guesses

Every AI-detected chord, note, and tempo carries a visible confidence score — so you always know what's a fact and what's Dawsons' best guess.

How it's built

  • Local-first. Your audio and projects stay on your machine.
  • Fully free stack. Every dependency is free to use commercially — no paid APIs, no subscriptions passed on to you.
  • Open engineering. The full source and roadmap are public on GitHub.
